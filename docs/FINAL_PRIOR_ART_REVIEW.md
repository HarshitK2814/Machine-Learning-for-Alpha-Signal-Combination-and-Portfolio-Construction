# Prior-art and bibliography review (2026-10-14, sprint 2)

Internal researcher-led review; not an external review. Method: lawful, publicly reachable sources only (publisher and repository pages, Crossref and arXiv records, author or institutional PDFs). Where a PDF was reachable it was downloaded to a scratch directory, text-extracted locally and searched; comparisons below rest only on text actually read. **A work is marked "full text read" only if its body text was read in the version named, which is often a working-paper or author version, not the published article.** Nothing was paid for, no access was circumvented, and nothing was inferred from memory or from general knowledge: where a source could not be read the limitation is stated. Credentials were not used. Metadata fetched through a summarising web tool were taken at face value from Crossref/arXiv records and recorded in `submission_candidate_as_run_only/BIB_PROVENANCE.csv`.

## 1. Closest prior art: Avramov, Cheng and Metzker (Management Science 2023)

* **Metadata verified:** Doron Avramov, Si Cheng, Lior Metzker, "Machine Learning vs. Economic Restrictions: Evidence from Stock Return Predictability", *Management Science* 69(5), 2587-2619, 2023, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2022.4449 (Hebrew University repository record of the publisher metadata).
* **Full text: NOT AVAILABLE.** SSRN (abstract 3450322) and INFORMS return HTTP 403; ResearchGate states no full text; no author or institutional copy was found; a 12-page Korean slide deck on a Seoul National University page is a presentation, not the paper, and was not used.
* **What the abstract establishes (abstract only):** deep-learning investment signals earn profits mainly from hard-to-arbitrage stocks and in high limits-to-arbitrage periods; excluding microcaps, distressed firms or volatile markets substantially reduces them; trading costs reduce them further because of high turnover and extreme positions; the signals remain profitable on long positions and in recent years and carry low downside risk.
* **Not established by what we read:** the paper's data, its cost model, whether it treats capacity or AUM, taxes, non-US markets, its robustness checks and its limitations. **No comparison of methods or limitations with our work can be made.** The candidate says so. Our reading that our net-Sharpe null "corroborates" it is an interpretation from the abstract and is worded as such. A detailed novelty claim against this paper is blocked until the full text is read.
