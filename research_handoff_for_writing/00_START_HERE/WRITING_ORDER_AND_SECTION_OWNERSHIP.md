# Writing order and section ownership

Ownership means who may change the content of the section's evidence. "Writer" is the collaborator writing the paper; "Research owner" is the person responsible for code, analysis, tables and figures. Nothing in this table gives anyone authority over author, venue or submission decisions; those stay with the authors.

| Order | Manuscript section | Writer may do | Research owner owns | Assets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Abstract and Section 1 (introduction) | Improve prose and flow; keep the four supported statements, the as-run qualification and the narrow contribution sentence | Every statement of fact and every number | none (text) |
| 2 | Section 2 (related work) | Rewrite the narrative; reorganise | Which papers were read in full and what they say; the novelty comparison (`06_LITERATURE/`) | none |
| 3 | Section 3 (data and markets) | Explain the panels and universe in plain language | Coverage numbers, the India 2009-10 disclosure, statute inputs | `TAB_M1`, `FIG_M2`, `TAB_M3`; supplement `FIG_S5-S7`, `TAB_S9-S11` |
| 4 | Section 4 (framework) | Make the cost model, books, solvency rule and inference description readable | Parameter values, definitions, inference method | `FIG_M1`, `TAB_M4`, `TAB_S6` |
| 5 | Section 5.1 (from signal to executable portfolio) | Narrate the table and figure | Every number | `TAB_M2`, `FIG_M3`, `TAB_S12-S14` |
| 6 | Section 5.2 (capacity) | Narrate; keep every "accounting difference, not a gain" caveat | B(A), solvency, comparator facts | `FIG_M4`, `TAB_S2`, `FIG_S1`, `FIG_S2`, `FIG_S15`, `TAB_S17` |
| 7 | Section 5.3 (tax) | Narrate | Tax channel, long-term share, statute facts | `TAB_M3`, `TAB_S1`, `TAB_S14`, `FIG_S20` |
| 8 | Section 5.4 (design axes) | Narrate | Power, minimum detectable effects, the exploratory Japan contrast | `TAB_S3`, `TAB_S20`, `FIG_S22` |
| 9 | Section 6 (coverage-asymmetry case) | Tighten | The worked-failure facts (retracted result) | none (no figure: retracted) |
| 10 | Section 7 (limitations) and Appendix A and B | Improve clarity; keep every item | The list itself | `TAB_S7`, `TAB_S8`, `TAB_S21`, `07_LIMITATIONS_AND_OPEN_ITEMS/` |
| 11 | Section 8 (conclusion) | Prose | Consistency with the abstract | none |
| last | Title page, availability statements, acknowledgements, funding, conflicts | **Nothing**; these are placeholders for the authors | Authors | none |

Rules for every section: cite exhibits by asset ID; copy numbers from the CSV or Markdown table; keep "as-run" and "unknown exit-treatment sensitivity" wherever results are stated; do not write "pre-registered" or "confirmatory"; do not mention any approval by a co-author or reviewer.
