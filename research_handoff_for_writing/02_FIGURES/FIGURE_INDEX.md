## Captions and limitations

### FIG_M1_pipeline

* Status: `MAIN_TEXT_ELIGIBLE`; intended location: Section 4 (framework).
* Caption: Research design and as-run analysis pipeline. Certified panels from a separate data workstream feed the 2x2x2x2 model factorial, three portfolio books and a lot-level backtest under a zero-tax control and a statutory regime; committed snapshots are the only inputs to the tables and figures. Schematic: no data are plotted. The unrun and unknown items in the lower right are part of the design record.
* What it does and does not establish: Answers: what was run and what was not. No values, so nothing to trace. Placement: main text as a design record; it must not be read as a result.

### FIG_M2_cost_input_coverage

* Status: `MAIN_TEXT_ELIGIBLE`; intended location: Section 3 (data).
* Caption: Share of in-universe security-months with certified cost inputs, by month, 2009-01 to 2019-12. Germany's investable universe is smaller than its signal-bearing one because uncertified security-months are non-tradeable and not imputed. This describes input support; it is not a performance result.
* What it does and does not establish: Answers: how much of the universe is tradeable on certified inputs. Definition identical across markets (certified over in-universe, per month). No uncertainty needed: a count ratio. The one-month India collapse is annotated from the data, not smoothed. Placement: main text.

### FIG_M3_sharpe_ladder_channels

* Status: `MAIN_TEXT_ELIGIBLE`; intended location: Section 5.1.
* Caption: Capped decile composite at $1bn. (a) Gross, net and after-tax annualised Sharpe ratios by market. (b) Cost channel (net minus gross) and tax channel (after-tax minus net) in Sharpe points. Comparisons use matched books and identical definitions in all three markets. No interval is drawn because the source holds none; no net Sharpe is significantly positive. The non-executable uncapped Japanese comparator is not plotted.
* What it does and does not establish: Answers: where gross predictability goes under costs and tax. Same book (capped decile composite), same AUM, same definitions in all three markets. No interval is drawn because none is in the source; the figure does not claim significance. The executable and the non-executable books are not mixed. Placement: main text.

### FIG_M4_capacity_B_tierB

* Status: `MAIN_TEXT_ELIGIBLE`; intended location: Section 5.2 (candidate Figure 1).
* Caption: B(A), the annualised mean net-return difference between capped and uncapped decile composites (Tier B), by mandate size, solvent rungs only, with unadjusted 95% bootstrap intervals. B(A) is an accounting difference against an infeasible, often near-ruined comparator; it is not realised performance, not investable, and not convertible to Sharpe points. Insolvent rungs are shaded and carry no B(A).
* What it does and does not establish: Answers: how the capped-minus-uncapped accounting difference grows with mandate size. Intervals are the source's unadjusted bootstrap intervals; insolvent rungs are shaded and unplotted; near-ruined comparators are hollow. Caption states B(A) is not investable performance. Placement: main text (already Figure 1 of the candidate, restyled), as an accounting illustration.
